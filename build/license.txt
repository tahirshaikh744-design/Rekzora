1. PRIVACY POLICY
========================================================================

1.1 THE SHORT VERSION
Rekzora is an offline-first application. We collect nothing. We track
nobody. We phone no home. There is no account, no login, no cloud, no
analytics and no advertising of any kind inside Rekzora.

1.2 WHAT DATA REKZORA STORES, AND WHERE
All user data produced by Rekzora stays on YOUR computer, in two places:

  a) Your Recordings
     Video files (WebM) you create are saved to the folder YOU choose
     (by default the "Rekzora Recordings" folder in your Videos library).
     We never upload, transmit or copy these files anywhere. Only you,
     or apps you explicitly drop/share them into, can move them off
     your machine.

  b) Application Preferences
     A small local settings file and a recordings index are stored in
     your own Windows AppData profile folder
     (C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Roaming\Rekzora\).
     This holds your theme, frame rate, quality, hotkeys, save folder
     and thumbnail metadata so the app remembers your setup. It never
     leaves your device unless Windows Backup or you copy it.
     Interrupted recordings are additionally kept as temporary files in
     the same folder tree (Rekzora\recordings-tmp) together with a small
     manifest, so Rekzora can offer them to you on the next launch
     (recovery). They stay on your device until you keep or delete them,
     or until the app finds an empty temp file with no matching data
     (then it cleans that pair quietly).

1.3 WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT
 - No personal information (name, address, contact details)
 - No audio, video or screen content (it is never streamed, uploaded,
   or processed outside your device memory)
 - No crash dumps are transmitted automatically
 - No telemetry, analytics, statistics, or usage tracking of any kind
 - No cookies, fingerprinting, or advertising identifiers
 - No purchase/billing data (Rekzora has no paid accounts)

1.4 NETWORK ACCESS
Rekzora does not create or require network connections as part of normal
operation. The only networking that can ever occur is initiated and
performed BY YOU, outside Rekzora's code — for example when you click a
link that opens your browser, or when you choose to transmit a video
using YOUR installed sharing applications (Google Quick Share, SHAREit,
LocalSend, Send Anywhere, Xender, Telegram, etc.). When you use those
applications you are subject to THEIR privacy policies, not ours.

1.5 MICROPHONE, CAMERA AND SCREEN
Rekzora can only use your microphone, webcam and screen-faces-captured
content while a recording is actively running, and only when you (or
your recorded hotkey) started it. Windows may display recording
indicators during that time. Recordings are written straight to disk;
Rekzora does not keep hidden copies.

1.6 CHILDREN
Rekzora is a generic desktop utility and is not directed at children
under 13. Because no data is collected, no data about children exists.

1.7 YOUR RIGHTS & DATA DELETION
Because all data already lives on your own device, you are always in
full control:
 - Uninstalling Rekzora (or deleting the portable ZIP/exe) removes the
   program. Delete the recordings folder to erase recordings.
 - "Delete recordings from disk" inside Rekzora permanently erases the
   selected videos from your drive (no recycle bin).
 - Ticking "Delete my Rekzora data when uninstalling" (where shown) also
   removes preferences from AppData.

1.8 CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If this policy ever changes (e.g. an optional update checker is added),
changes will be listed here and flagged inside the app before any new
behavior activates. No change will silently start collecting data.
