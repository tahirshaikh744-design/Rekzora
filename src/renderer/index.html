Privacy Policy & Terms of Use
Rekzora · NEURON TS LABS — Mohd Tahir Shaikh
NEURON TS LABS

1. Privacy — the short version. Rekzora is offline-first. We collect nothing, track nobody, and phone no home. There is no account, no login, no cloud, no analytics and no advertising.

2. What stays on your PC. Your recordings are saved only to the folder you choose (by default Videos\Rekzora Recordings), and your preferences live in your own AppData profile. Nothing is uploaded, streamed or copied anywhere — ever.

3. What we do NOT collect. No personal information. No audio, video or screen content — recordings never leave your device memory except when you share them yourself. No crash dumps sent automatically. No telemetry, statistics or usage tracking. No cookies, fingerprinting or ad identifiers.

4. Network access. Rekzora makes no network connections of its own. Any transmission (sharing a video via Quick Share, SHAREit, Telegram…) is performed by your apps, under their privacy policies — not ours.

5. Microphone, camera & screen. Used only while a recording you started is actively running. Windows shows recording indicators at that time. Recordings go straight to your disk; no hidden copies are kept.

6. Your rights & deletion. Because all data is already local, you are always in control: uninstalling removes the program, deleting the recordings folder erases your videos, and in-app Delete permanently removes selected files (no recycle bin).
